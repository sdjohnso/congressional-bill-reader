R. 1225 To direct the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP Code for Eastvale, California. _______________________________________________________________________ IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES February 12, 2025 Mr. Calvert (for himself and Mrs. Torres of California) introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Oversight and Government Reform _______________________________________________________________________ A BILL To direct the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP Code for Eastvale, California. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, SECTION 1. SINGLE, UNIQUE ZIP CODE FOR EASTVALE, CALIFORNIA. Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this section, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP Code applicable to the area encompassing solely Eastvale, California. &lt;all&gt;
